Why SKIT

About us

Built for engineers who like to build

SKIT was founded in 2000 with a simple idea: an engineer learns fastest with a tool in hand, not just a textbook.

Our mission

To train engineers who can design, build, and troubleshoot real systems — not just pass exams — while keeping the campus itself a working example of sustainable design.

Our vision

To be recognised as the region's most hands-on B.Tech college, where every graduate leaves with a working prototype in their portfolio.
